{%- comment -%} Every site page starts with this: L is the page's language (its `lang`, English when it has none), T its words (_data/lang/.yml), H what stays the same in every language (_data/home.yml). A city's name in L is {{ T.cities[c.id].name | default: c.name }}, its country and card line the same; its page {% include city-url.html city=c.id %}. Q and QA go after a link to the game (?lang=fr, &lang=fr after another parameter), so that it starts in L; nothing in English. On a page of a city (a `city`): C its block of _data/cities.yml, CN its name in L, DE "of CN" (%d in _data/lang/.yml: French "de Gand", "d'Avignon"), CT its page's words (_data/i18n//.yml), GT its guide's (.../guide/.yml) and LMT its landmarks' (.../landmarks/.yml), each nothing in English, where the words are the data's own. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign L = page.lang | default: 'en' -%} {%- assign T = site.data.lang[L] -%} {%- assign H = site.data.home -%} {%- if L == 'en' %}{% assign Q = '' %}{% assign QA = '' %}{% else %}{% assign Q = '?lang=' | append: L %}{% assign QA = '&lang=' | append: L %}{% endif -%} {%- if page.city -%} {%- assign C = site.data.cities | where: 'id', page.city | first -%} {%- assign CN = T.cities[C.id].name | default: C.name -%} {%- assign DE = CN -%}{%- if L == 'fr' %}{% assign cn1 = CN | slice: 0 | downcase %}{% if 'aeiouyh' contains cn1 %}{% assign DE = 'd’' | append: CN %}{% else %}{% assign DE = 'de ' | append: CN %}{% endif %}{% endif -%} {%- assign CT = site.data.i18n[L][C.id] -%}{%- assign GT = site.data.i18n[L].guide[C.id] -%}{%- assign LMT = site.data.i18n[L].landmarks[C.id] -%} {%- endif -%}
