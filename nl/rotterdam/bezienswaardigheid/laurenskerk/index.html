Laurenskerk

Grote of Sint-Laurenskerk

De grote laatgotische kerk van de stad, in 1940 uitgebrand en herbouwd, het laatste middeleeuwse gebouw van het centrum.

Photo: Michielverbeek, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Grote of Sint-Laurenskerk was built from 1449 to 1525, the parish church of the medieval town, with a heavy square tower. In the bombing of 1940 it burned out, but its walls and tower stood, and it was rebuilt by 1968. It is the only medieval building left in the centre of Rotterdam.

Inside are big organs and the tombs of Dutch admirals. On the square in front stands Hendrick de Keyser’s statue of Erasmus, born in Rotterdam, reading a book; cast in 1622, it is the oldest bronze statue in the Netherlands. Bezoek het in 3D ›
