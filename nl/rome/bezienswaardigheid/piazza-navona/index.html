Piazza Navona

Een lang barok plein op de omtrek van het stadion van Domitianus, met de Vierstromenfontein van Bernini.

Bekijk het in 3D Bekijk de rondleiding

Photo: NikonZ7II, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Piazza Navona keeps the exact shape of the stadium Emperor Domitian built here in the 1st century for athletic contests; the houses stand on its old stands. In the 17th century Pope Innocent X made it a showpiece of Baroque Rome.

In the middle stands Bernini’s Fountain of the Four Rivers, with the Nile, the Ganges, the Danube and the Río de la Plata around an obelisk. Facing it is Borromini’s church of Sant’Agnese in Agone, his great rival’s work. Bezoek het in 3D ›
