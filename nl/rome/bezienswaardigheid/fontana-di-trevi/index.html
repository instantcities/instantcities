Trevifontein

De grootste fontein van Rome: wie een munt over zijn schouder gooit, komt terug.

Photo: NikonZ7II, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Trevi Fountain is the end of the Aqua Virgo, an aqueduct from 19 BC that still brings water to the city. Nicola Salvi designed the fountain against the back of a palace, and it was finished in 1762: Oceanus rides his shell chariot out of a triumphal arch, pulled by sea horses and tritons over a cliff of rough stone.

Tradition says that a coin thrown over your shoulder into the basin brings you back to Rome. Thousands of euros are fished out every week and given to charity. Bezoek het in 3D ›
