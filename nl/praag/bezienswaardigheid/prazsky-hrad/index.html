Prague Castle

Pražský hrad

The castle on the hill across the river, the largest ancient castle complex in the world; beyond this map.

Speel Praag Bekijk de rondleiding

Photo: Tilman2007, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Prague Castle has been the seat of Bohemian kings, Holy Roman emperors and Czech presidents since the 9th century. Within its walls stand St Vitus Cathedral, palaces, churches and the little Golden Lane.

It lies across the Vltava from the Old Town, beyond the piece of Prague the game has so far; you see it from the river. Rijd door Praag in 3D ›
