On the map

  1. 1

    🏛️ Parthenon

    The great temple of Athena has eight columns at each end and seventeen along each side.

  2. 2

    🏛️ Erechtheion

    An asymmetrical temple north of the Parthenon, known for its porch of six Caryatids.

  3. 3

    🏛️ Propylaea

    The monumental western gateway frames the approach to the summit.

  4. 4

    🏛️ Temple of Athena Nike

    A small Ionic temple stands beside the western entrance.
