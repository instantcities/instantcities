Grand Canal

Canal Grande

La grande voie d’eau en forme de S au milieu de Venise, bordée de palais.

Voir en 3D Voir la visite

Photo: Didier Descouens, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Grand Canal winds nearly four kilometres through the city in a reversed S, lined by some 170 palaces built over five centuries by the city’s great families, their doors opening onto the water.

It is Venice’s main street: vaporetti, water taxis, delivery barges and gondolas all share it, and only four bridges cross it. Le visiter en 3D ›
