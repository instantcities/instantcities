# Instant Cities: App Store texts

Every text App Store Connect asks for, in the order its pages ask for it, ready to copy. Open the
app at App Store Connect → Apps → Instant Cities
(<https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps/6826871341/distribution>). Each text sits in its own block,
so the block's copy button takes exactly that field. The store page is in English (U.K.) only, for now.
How a new build goes up is in `../README.md`.

## 3. Version page

**Promotional Text** (94/170)

```text
24 European cities in 3D, from Paris and Rome to Ghent and Delft. Free, no ads, works offline.
```

**Description**

```text
Ride a bike, walk or gallop through the real streets of 24 European cities, built in 3D from OpenStreetMap.

Pick a city from the list: Paris, Rome, London, Venice, Barcelona, Amsterdam, Florence, Prague, Vienna, Berlin, Madrid, Copenhagen, Brussels, Bruges, Antwerp, Avignon, Salerno, Siena, Pisa, Utrecht, Rotterdam, Delft, Lecce and Ghent.

In each city you can:
• ride around freely, as long as you like
• race along a route through the centre
• take the tour, which flies past the sights and says a few words about each
• play a short mission against the clock

The streets, the water and the buildings come from OpenStreetMap. The best-known landmarks are modelled by hand.

The app is free, has no ads and no account, and collects nothing about you. Every city is inside the app, so it also plays without internet. The game speaks English, Dutch, French, German, Spanish and Italian.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
```

## 4. App Privacy

**Privacy Policy URL**

```text
https://instantcities.net/privacy/
```

**Data Collection** → Get Started → "No, we do not collect data from this app". The app has no
analytics, no account and no network use; its settings stay on the device. Export compliance
needs no answer: the build says it uses no non-exempt encryption.

## 6. App Review Information

- Sign-in required: **no**.
- Contact: Wouter's name, phone and e-mail (only Apple's reviewers see them).

**Notes**

```text
Instant Cities is a free 3D game set in real European cities. No account or sign-in is needed, and the app works fully offline: every city is inside the app.

To play: pick a city from the list, wait for it to load, then tap "Tap to ride" at the bottom of the menu. Drag on the screen to steer. The tabs in the menu switch between missions, a race and the tour, which flies through the city on its own. The "Cities" button in the menu goes back to the list.

The cities are built from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).
```
