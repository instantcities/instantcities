New York Public Library

The marble library on Fifth Avenue, guarded by the two stone lions Patience and Fortitude.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: (vincent desjardins) from Paris, France, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Stephen A. Schwarzman Building, the main building of the New York Public Library, opened in 1911 on the site of an old reservoir. Carrère and Hastings built it of white marble in the Beaux-Arts style, and it was the largest marble building in the United States at the time.

Two stone lions lie at the foot of its steps on Fifth Avenue. In the 1930s Mayor Fiorello La Guardia named them Patience and Fortitude, the qualities he said New Yorkers would need to get through the Depression. Upstairs the Rose Main Reading Room, under a painted ceiling, is open to anyone who wants to read. Visit it in 3D ›
