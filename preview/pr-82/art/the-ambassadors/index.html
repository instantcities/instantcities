The Ambassadors

Hans Holbein the Younger, 1533

Two young Frenchmen at the court of Henry VIII, between them a table of globes and instruments, and a skull across the floor.

See it in 3D The National Gallery London

Jean de Dinteville, the French ambassador in London, and his friend Georges de Selve, a bishop, stand either side of a shelf of things for measuring the heavens and the earth: globes, sundials, a quadrant, a lute with a broken string.

The grey smear across the floor is a skull, painted in steep perspective: it only takes its shape when you look from close to the frame on the right. Death waits behind all that learning. The National Gallery bought it in 1890. Stand before it in 3D ›
