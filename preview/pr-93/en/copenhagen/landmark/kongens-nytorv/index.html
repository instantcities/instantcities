Kongens Nytorv

The King’s New Square, with the Royal Danish Theatre and a bronze king on horseback.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Jim G, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Kongens Nytorv was laid out in 1670 by King Christian V, whose bronze statue on horseback stands in the middle. Around it stand the Royal Danish Theatre, the Hotel d’Angleterre and the Charlottenborg palace.

It is the start of the Strøget and the gateway to Nyhavn. Visit it in 3D ›
