Meir

Antwerp’s great shopping street, lined with palaces and department stores.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: G.Lanting, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Meir runs from the old town towards the station, lined with grand houses, among them the 18th-century Paleis op de Meir, where Napoleon stayed. Today it is Belgium’s busiest shopping street.

More in Antwerp

Grote MarktThe square of the city hall and the guild houses, with the Brabo fountain in the middle. Brabo FountainThe Roman soldier Brabo throwing the giant’s hand away: the legend behind the city’s name. City HallA Renaissance city hall from 1565, all flags and columns, on the Grote Markt. Cathedral of Our LadyThe largest Gothic church in the Low Countries, its spire 123 metres high, with four Rubens altarpieces. Het SteenThe oldest building in Antwerp, a castle on the Scheldt from the Middle Ages. VleeshuisThe Gothic hall of the butchers’ guild, striped in brick and white stone. GroenplaatsThe square at the foot of the cathedral, with a statue of Rubens in the middle. Plantin-MoretusThe house and workshop of a great printing firm, with the two oldest printing presses in the world. RubenshuisThe house and studio where Peter Paul Rubens lived and painted from 1610. DiamondsAntwerp has traded and cut diamonds since the 15th century; most of the world’s rough diamonds still pass through it. Antwerp handsBiscuits and chocolates in the shape of a hand, after the Brabo legend.
