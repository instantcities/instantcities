Sablon

The square of antique dealers and chocolatiers, below the Gothic church of Notre-Dame du Sablon.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Rolf Kranz, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Grand Sablon is lined with antique shops and the windows of Brussels’ grandest chocolate makers. On weekends an antiques market fills it.

Above it stands the late-Gothic church of Notre-Dame du Sablon, famous for its tall windows; across the road lies the little garden of the Petit Sablon with its 48 bronze statues of the old guilds. Visit it in 3D ›
