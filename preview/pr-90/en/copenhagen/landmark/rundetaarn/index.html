Round Tower

Christian IV’s observatory of 1642, climbed by a spiral ramp instead of stairs.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Aram Zucker-Scharff from Fairfax, VA, USA, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Rundetaarn was built in the 1640s as an observatory for the university. Instead of stairs, a broad spiral ramp winds up inside, seven and a half turns, wide enough for a horse and carriage.

Peter the Great of Russia is said to have ridden up it on horseback in 1716. At the top is the oldest working observatory in Europe. Visit it in 3D ›
