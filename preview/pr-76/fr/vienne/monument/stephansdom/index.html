Cathédrale Saint-Étienne

Stephansdom

La cathédrale gothique de Vienne, avec sa tour sud de 136 mètres et un toit de tuiles vernissées en zigzag.

Voir en 3D Voir la visite

Photo: Dietmar Rabich, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Stephansdom was rebuilt in Gothic style from the 14th century on; its south tower, the Steffl, finished in 1433, rises 136 metres. The north tower was never finished and got a little Renaissance cap.

Its roof of some 230,000 glazed tiles shows the double-headed eagle and the arms of Vienna. In 1945 fire destroyed the roof; it was rebuilt by 1952 with help from all over Austria. Le visiter en 3D ›
