Monuments

Cathédrale Saint-ÉtienneLa cathédrale gothique de Vienne, avec sa tour sud de 136 mètres et un toit de tuiles vernissées en zigzag. GrabenLa grande rue commerçante de Vienne, sur le tracé du fossé romain, avec la colonne de la peste au milieu. Colonne de la pesteLa colonne baroque de nuages et d’anges dressée après la peste de 1679. HofburgLe palais d’hiver des Habsbourg pendant six siècles, aujourd’hui siège du président autrichien. MichaelerplatzLa place ronde devant la porte de la Hofburg, où des ruines romaines et la maison sobre d’Adolf Loos font face au palais. École d’équitation espagnoleLes étalons lipizzans blancs dansent dans un manège baroque à l’intérieur de la Hofburg. Opéra d’ÉtatInauguré en 1869, le premier grand édifice du Ring et l’un des plus grands opéras du monde. AlbertinaUn palais des Habsbourg abritant l’une des plus grandes collections d’estampes et de dessins au monde, dont le Lièvre de Dürer. BurgtheaterL’un des plus importants théâtres de langue allemande, sur le Ring, en face de l’hôtel de ville. Kunsthistorisches MuseumThe Habsburgs’ art museum on Maria-Theresien-Platz, with the largest collection of Bruegel’s paintings in the world.

Rues, places et cours d’eau

Les lieux qui tiennent ensemble le centre de Vienne. Pointez-en un pour le trouver sur la carte.

  1. 1
    Stephansplatz place

    The square around St Stephen’s Cathedral, where the Graben, Kärntner Straße and Rotenturmstraße meet. Under it lies the Virgilkapelle, a medieval chapel that builders found in 1973 while digging the metro.

  2. 2
    Graben rue

    A wide pedestrian street on the line of the ditch of the Roman fort, which gave it its name. The Baroque Plague Column stands in the middle.

  3. 3
    Kärntner Straße rue

    The busy shopping street from St Stephen’s to the State Opera, named after Carinthia, Kärnten, the province the old road led to.

  4. 4
    Neuer Markt place

    A square off Kärntner Straße with the Donner Fountain and the Capuchin Church, whose crypt holds the tombs of the Habsburgs.

  5. 5
    Albertinaplatz place

    The square below the Albertina, behind the State Opera, with Alfred Hrdlicka’s memorial against war and fascism.

  6. 6
    Kohlmarkt rue

    The short, elegant street from the Graben to the Hofburg, named after the charcoal once sold there, with jewellers, fashion houses and the confectioner Demel.

  7. 7
    Michaelerplatz place

    The round square in front of the Hofburg’s domed Michael Gate, with Roman remains in its middle and the plain Looshaus, which shocked the Viennese in 1911.

  8. 8
    Josefsplatz place

    A courtyard-like square of the Hofburg with an equestrian statue of Joseph II, in front of the Baroque State Hall of the National Library.

  9. 9
    Heroes’ Square Heldenplatz place

    The great open square of the Hofburg, with statues of Prince Eugene and Archduke Charles on horseback. From the balcony of the New Castle Hitler proclaimed the annexation of Austria here in 1938.

  10. 10
    The Ringstraße Ringstraße rue

    The boulevard around the old town that took the place of the city walls from 1857, lined with the Opera, the Parliament, the City Hall and the great museums.

  11. 11
    Herrengasse rue

    The street of the nobles’ town palaces, from the Michaelerplatz to the Freyung, with the old parliament of Lower Austria and the Café Central.

  12. 12
    Am Hof place

    The largest square of the old town, where the Babenberg dukes kept their court. From the balcony of its church a herald proclaimed the end of the Holy Roman Empire in 1806.

  13. 13
    Judenplatz place

    The centre of the medieval Jewish quarter, destroyed in 1421, with Rachel Whiteread’s Holocaust memorial, a library cast in concrete with its books turned inside out.

  14. 14
    Hoher Markt place

    One of Vienna’s oldest squares, above the remains of the Roman fort, with the Ankeruhr, an Art Nouveau clock across which historical figures parade at noon.

  15. 15
    The Danube Canal Donaukanal cours d’eau

    The arm of the Danube along the north-east edge of the old town; the main river flows further out. In summer bars and deckchairs fill its banks.
