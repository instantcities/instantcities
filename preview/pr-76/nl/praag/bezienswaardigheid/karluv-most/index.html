Karelsbrug

Karlův most

De stenen brug uit 1357 over de Moldau, met dertig heiligenbeelden langs de rand.

Bekijk het in 3D Bekijk de rondleiding

Photo: Chosovi ; Diligent cropped the original picture a bit., CC BY-SA 2.5, via Wikimedia Commons

Charles IV laid the bridge’s first stone in 1357, at 5:31 on 9 July, a time chosen for its palindrome of numbers: 1357 9 7 5 31. For four centuries it was the only bridge across the Vltava in Prague.

From the 17th century thirty statues of saints were put along its parapets. Touch the bronze relief of St John of Nepomuk, thrown from the bridge in 1393, and you will come back to Prague. Bezoek het in 3D ›
