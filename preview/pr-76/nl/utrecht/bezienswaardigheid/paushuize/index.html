Paushuize

Het huis aan de Kromme Nieuwegracht, gebouwd voor Adriaan van Utrecht, de enige Nederlandse paus.

Bekijk het in 3D Bekijk de rondleiding

Photo: China Crisis, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Adriaan Florensz, born in Utrecht in 1459, taught at Leuven and tutored the young emperor Charles V. He had this house built for when he would come home, but in 1522 he was elected pope as Adrian VI, the only Dutchman ever to be pope.

He died in Rome the next year and never lived in it. The house now belongs to the province, which uses it for receptions. Bezoek het in 3D ›
