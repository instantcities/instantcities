On the map

  1. 1

    🔺 The Great Pyramid

    The tomb of King Khufu, about 2560 BC. It stands 230 metres a side and is 139 metres high now. Some 2.3 million blocks of limestone make it up, and it was the tallest building on Earth until Lincoln Cathedral passed it around 1300.

  2. 2

    ⛰️ The pyramid of Khafre

    Khufu’s son’s pyramid is 136 metres high. It stands on higher ground, so it looks taller than the Great Pyramid. The smooth limestone casing that once covered all of them is still on its top.

  3. 3

    🔻 The pyramid of Menkaure

    The smallest of the three, 65 metres high, with its lower courses faced in red granite from Aswan. Its builders died before it was finished, and the casing was never smoothed.

  4. 4

    👑 The queens’ pyramids

    Three small pyramids next to the Great Pyramid, built for the wives and family of Khufu. Each was about 30 metres high; now they are broken off at the top.

  5. 5

    🦁 The Great Sphinx

    A lion with the head of a king, probably Khafre, carved from the bedrock about 2500 BC. It is 73 metres long and 20 high, and it looks due east. Sand buried it to the chest for most of its history.

  6. 6

    🏘️ Nazlet el-Semman

    The town at the foot of the plateau, right up against the Sphinx. The pyramids were once out in the desert; now a street of houses runs to the edge of the site.
