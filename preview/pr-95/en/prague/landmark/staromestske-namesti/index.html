Old Town Square

Staroměstské náměstí

The great square of the Old Town, with the Týn church, the astronomical clock and the Jan Hus memorial.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Diliff, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Old Town Square has been Prague’s market place since the 12th century. Around it stand the Old Town Hall with its clock, the Baroque church of St Nicholas, pastel palaces and the two spiky towers of the Týn church.

In the middle stands the memorial to Jan Hus, the reformer burned at the stake in 1415, unveiled in 1915. Twenty-seven white crosses in the paving mark where 27 Czech leaders were executed in 1621. Visit it in 3D ›
