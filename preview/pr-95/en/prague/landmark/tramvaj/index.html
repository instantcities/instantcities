Prague has one of the largest tram networks in Europe, with red and cream trams running day and night. The old Tatra T3, rounded and boxy, ran here for decades and still runs on a few lines.

Line 17 runs along the river past the Rudolfinum, one of the most beautiful rides in the city. Ride through Prague in 3D ›

More in Prague

Old Town SquareThe great square of the Old Town, with the Týn church, the astronomical clock and the Jan Hus memorial. Astronomical clockMade in 1410: every hour the twelve apostles pass its little windows while Death rings his bell. Týn ChurchThe Gothic church with two spiky 80-metre towers, behind the houses on the Old Town Square. Charles BridgeThe stone bridge of 1357 across the Vltava, lined with thirty statues of saints. Old Town Bridge TowerThe Gothic gate tower at the Old Town end of the Charles Bridge, one of the finest in Europe. KlementinumThe vast Jesuit college beside the bridge, with a Baroque library hall and an astronomical tower. JosefovPrague’s Jewish Quarter, with old synagogues and the Old Jewish Cemetery. Old-New SynagogueBuilt around 1270, the oldest synagogue in Europe still in use. RudolfinumThe neo-Renaissance concert hall on the Vltava, home of the Czech Philharmonic. Powder TowerA Gothic gate tower of 1475 at the start of the Royal Route, once used to store gunpowder. Prague CastleThe castle on the hill across the river, the largest ancient castle complex in the world; beyond this map. Czech beerCzechs drink more beer per person than anyone; the pilsner was born in Bohemia.
