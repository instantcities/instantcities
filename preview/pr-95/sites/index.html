Famous sites

Places that are not cities, from temples to tombs, each with a race, a tour and a few quests.

The map comes from OpenStreetMap where it has one, and the monuments are modelled by hand.

Taj Mahal Agra, India

The tomb with its dome and four minarets at the end of the long canal, between the mosque and the Jawab.
