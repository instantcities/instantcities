Cathedral

Catedral de Barcelona

The Gothic cathedral of Santa Eulàlia, with thirteen white geese in its cloister.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Josep Renalias, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Cathedral of the Holy Cross and Saint Eulalia was built from the end of the 13th century to the 15th; its neo-Gothic front only followed in the late 19th century. Saint Eulalia, a girl martyred in Roman times, lies in its crypt.

In the cloister, among palms and a fountain, live thirteen white geese, one for each year of her life. Visit it in 3D ›
