Sampietrini

The small black cobblestones of basalt that pave the streets of old Rome.

Jouer à Rome Voir la visite

Photo: Alessio Damato, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Sampietrini are small cubes of dark volcanic stone, set by hand in sand. They are named after Saint Peter’s Square, where they were first laid on a large scale in the 16th century.

They are beautiful, cheap to mend and terrible for scooters and high heels. In the game the streets of the old centre are cobbled with them. Parcourir Rome en 3D ›
