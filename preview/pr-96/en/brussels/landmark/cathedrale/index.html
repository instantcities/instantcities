Cathedral of St Michael and St Gudula

Cathédrale Saints-Michel-et-Gudule

Brussels’ Gothic cathedral on the slope towards the upper town, where royal weddings are held.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The cathedral was built over some three centuries, from 1226, in the Gothic style of Brabant, with two square towers on its white front. Its stained-glass windows include ones given by Charles V.

It is where Belgian royal weddings and state funerals take place, and where the national Te Deum is sung. Visit it in 3D ›
