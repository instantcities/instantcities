What you can do

The monuments

Three pyramids rise above the desert, with smaller queens’ pyramids, ruined temples and the Sphinx on the eastern side.

The plateau circuit

Follow the marked circuit, or leave the race to explore freely. Play alone or with two players on one keyboard.

A tour and a quest

Take a flight over the site with short explanations of its landmarks, or find your walking group before the tour leaves.

On the map

  1. 1

    🔺 Great Pyramid of Khufu

    The largest of the three main pyramids. Most of its smooth outer casing has been lost.

  2. 2

    🔺 Pyramid of Khafre

    The second great pyramid retains pale casing stones near its summit.

  3. 3

    🔺 Great Sphinx

    A limestone lion with a human head guards the eastern side of the plateau.

  4. 4

    🔺 Pyramid of Menkaure

    The smallest of the three main pyramids stands beside three much smaller pyramids.
