On the map

  1. 1

    💧 The moat

    A moat 190 metres wide runs right round the temple, 5.5 kilometres in all. It stands for the ocean round Mount Meru, home of the gods, and it kept the temple’s ground damp and firm.

  2. 2

    🚪 The western gopura

    The long gate in the outer wall, with three towers. Elephants could walk through the middle. Behind it a stone road runs 350 metres to the temple.

  3. 3

    📜 The libraries

    Two small buildings on either side of the road, called libraries. Nobody knows what they held: scrolls, or sacred objects.

  4. 4

    🐍 The Terrace of Honour

    A cross-shaped terrace in front of the temple, where the king is thought to have met the people. Carved nagas, seven-headed snakes, guard the road.

  5. 5

    🖼️ The galleries

    The outer gallery is 800 metres long and carved with scenes from Hindu epics and the king’s battles: the longest stone relief in the world. Two more galleries rise above it.

  6. 6

    🛕 Angkor Wat

    Built in the early 1100s for the Khmer king Suryavarman II, first as a Hindu temple and later a Buddhist one. Three levels rise to five towers, the middle one 65 metres high, in the shape of the mountain of the gods.

About Angkor Wat

Built
Early 12th century, for the Khmer king Suryavarman II, first as a Hindu temple and later a Buddhist one
Size
The moat is 190 metres wide and the outer wall encloses about 200 hectares
Towers
Five, in a quincunx; the middle one is 65 metres high
In the game
The moat and the causeway, the western gopura and the temple's three levels with their galleries and towers
