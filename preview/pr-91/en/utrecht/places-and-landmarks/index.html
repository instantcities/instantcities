Places and landmarks in Utrecht

12 landmarks of Utrecht, and the streets, squares and waterways around them, each with its story and where to find it.

Landmarks

Dom TowerThe tallest church tower in the Netherlands, 112 metres of Gothic brick and stone, standing on its own since a storm blew down the nave. Dom ChurchWhat is left of Utrecht’s Gothic cathedral: the choir and transept, cut off from their tower since the nave fell in 1674. PandhofThe quiet cloister garden beside the Dom Church, planted with the herbs of a medieval monastery garden. OudegrachtUtrecht’s medieval canal, with its quays down at the water and the old cellars under the street now full of cafés. JanskerkhofThe leafy square round the Janskerk, with a flower market every Saturday. PieterskerkOne of the oldest churches in the Netherlands, a Romanesque church of 1048 with a crypt under its choir. Museum SpeelklokA museum of self-playing music, from music boxes to great dance organs, inside the medieval Buurkerk. AcademiegebouwThe main building of Utrecht University on the Domplein, where the Union of Utrecht was signed in 1579. PaushuizeThe house on the Kromme Nieuwegracht built for Adrian of Utrecht, who became the only Dutch pope. Museum CatharijneconventThe national museum of Christian art and history, in a medieval convent on the Oudegracht. Centraal MuseumThe oldest city museum in the Netherlands, with the Utrecht painters of the Golden Age and the furniture of Gerrit Rietveld.
