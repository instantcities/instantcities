TV Tower

Fernsehturm

East Berlin’s 368-metre TV tower of 1969, with a ball that throws a cross of light in the sun.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

East Germany built the Fernsehturm in 1969 to show off its capital: at 368 metres it is still the tallest building in Germany. Its steel ball holds a viewing floor and a restaurant that turns round once every hour.

When the sun shines on the ball, its panels reflect a cross of light, which Berliners called the Pope’s revenge. Visit it in 3D ›
