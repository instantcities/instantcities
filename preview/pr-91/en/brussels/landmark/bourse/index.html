Bourse

Bourse

The old stock exchange of 1873, on a boulevard that has been free of cars since 2015.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: iMahesh, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Bourse was built from 1868 to 1873 over the river Senne, which had just been covered over to make room for the grand boulevards. Its front of columns, statues and lions was meant to show off the wealth of young Belgium.

In 2015 the boulevard in front of it was closed to cars, and since 2023 the building has been the Belgian Beer World, a museum of beer. Visit it in 3D ›
