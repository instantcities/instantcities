House of Venus in the Shell

Casa della Venere in Conchiglia

A house whose garden wall is painted with Venus floating on a shell, with two cupids beside her.

Voir en 3D Art célèbre Voir la visite

Photo : Tyler Bell, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The house lies near the amphitheatre, round a garden with columns on two sides. Its back wall is covered by a large painting: Venus, the goddess of love, lies on a pink shell on the sea, with a cupid on either side.

On either side of her the wall is painted as a garden behind a fence, with birds and plants and a statue of Mars, so the real garden seemed to go on into the painted one. Le visiter en 3D ›

Histoire

Digging here began in 1933. In 1943, when the Allies bombed the area, a bomb hit the house, and the work was finished in 1952, when the painting of Venus came to light. It has been restored since.
