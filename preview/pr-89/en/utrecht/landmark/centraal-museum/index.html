Utrecht › Places and landmarks

Centraal Museum

The oldest city museum in the Netherlands, with the Utrecht painters of the Golden Age and the furniture of Gerrit Rietveld.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Centraal Museum / fotograaf: Vincent Zedelius, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Centraal Museum opened in 1838 and is the oldest municipal museum in the country. It has the work of the Utrecht Caravaggisti, the painters who brought Caravaggio’s light and dark back from Rome in the 1620s, and the largest collection of Gerrit Rietveld’s chairs and designs.

Rietveld’s Red Blue Chair is here, and his Schröder House, a World Heritage Site, lies on the edge of town. Visit it in 3D ›

More in Utrecht

Dom TowerThe tallest church tower in the Netherlands, 112 metres of Gothic brick and stone, standing on its own since a storm blew down the nave. Dom ChurchWhat is left of Utrecht’s Gothic cathedral: the choir and transept, cut off from their tower since the nave fell in 1674. PandhofThe quiet cloister garden beside the Dom Church, planted with the herbs of a medieval monastery garden. OudegrachtUtrecht’s medieval canal, with its quays down at the water and the old cellars under the street now full of cafés. JanskerkhofThe leafy square round the Janskerk, with a flower market every Saturday. PieterskerkOne of the oldest churches in the Netherlands, a Romanesque church of 1048 with a crypt under its choir. Museum SpeelklokA museum of self-playing music, from music boxes to great dance organs, inside the medieval Buurkerk. AcademiegebouwThe main building of Utrecht University on the Domplein, where the Union of Utrecht was signed in 1579. PaushuizeThe house on the Kromme Nieuwegracht built for Adrian of Utrecht, who became the only Dutch pope. Museum CatharijneconventThe national museum of Christian art and history, in a medieval convent on the Oudegracht. MiffyDick Bruna’s little white rabbit, born in Utrecht in 1955, who even has her own traffic lights in the town.
