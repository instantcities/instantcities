Cave Canem

Beware of the dog

Unknown Roman mosaicist, 1st century AD

A black dog on a chain, teeth bared, and the words CAVE CANEM: beware of the dog.

See it in 3D The House of the Tragic Poet Pompeii

A black dog with a red collar strains at his chain on a white ground, his teeth bared, his ears back. Under him is written CAVE CANEM, beware of the dog. Guests stepped over him as they came in from the street; it was a warning and a joke at once.

It is still in place at the door of the house, behind a glass screen. Other houses in Pompeii had dogs at their doors too, and the plaster cast of a real one, chained in the House of Orpheus, was made in 1874. Stand before it in 3D ›
