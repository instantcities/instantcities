Garden of the Fugitives

Orto dei Fuggiaschi

A garden by the south walls where thirteen people died under the ash. Plaster casts show where and how they fell.

Voir en 3D Voir la visite

Photo : Sparrow (麻雀), CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The garden was a vineyard in the south-east of the town, near the Porta Nocera. Thirteen people, adults and children, tried to get out of the town this way during the eruption and died here, overcome by the hot cloud of gas and ash that swept over Pompeii.

The ash set hard round their bodies, which rotted away and left hollows. In 1863 the archaeologist Giuseppe Fiorelli thought of pouring plaster into such hollows, and the casts show the people as they lay. The casts of the garden lie where they were found, under glass. Le visiter en 3D ›

Histoire

The garden was excavated in 1961. Other casts, of people and of a dog, are kept in the Forum Granaries and shown in the town.

Dates

AD 79
Thirteen people die here in the eruption
1863
Fiorelli makes the first plaster casts in Pompeii
1961
The garden is excavated
