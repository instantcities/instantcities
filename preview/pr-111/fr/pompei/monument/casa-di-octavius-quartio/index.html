House of Octavius Quartio

Casa di Octavius Quartio

A house with a long garden of water channels, pergolas and fountains, almost the length of its block.

Voir en 3D Art célèbre Voir la visite

Photo : Commonists, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The rooms of the house are few. What it is known for is the garden behind them: a long channel of water runs down its middle under a pergola, with fountains, small statues and a shrine along it, and a second channel crosses it at the top.

Next to the water stands a dining couch for eating outdoors, and on the walls near it are paintings of Narcissus looking at himself in a pool and of the lovers Pyramus and Thisbe. Le visiter en 3D ›

Histoire

The house was excavated from 1916 to 1921. It is also called the House of Loreius Tiburtinus, after a name painted outside; a seal ring found in it bears the name of Decimus Octavius Quartio. The garden has been replanted after the roots found in the ash.
