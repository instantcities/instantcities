Westerkerk

The church on the Prinsengracht with the tallest tower in Amsterdam, crowned by an imperial crown.

See it in 3D Watch the tour

Photo: Jules Verne Times Two, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The Westerkerk was finished in 1631, the largest Protestant church in the city at the time. Its 85-metre tower carries the imperial crown of Maximilian of Austria, which the city was allowed to use in its arms.

Rembrandt was buried here in 1669, in a grave now lost. Anne Frank could hear its bells from her hiding place round the corner. Visit it in 3D ›
